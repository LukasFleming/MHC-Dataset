# Experimental Dataset – Two-Stage Exchangeable Vessel Metal Hydride Compressor

## Overview
This repository contains the complete experimental dataset accompanying the paper

"Experimental Two-Stage Compressor with Exchangeable Metal Hydrides and Open Dataset Contribution"

The dataset documents the design, instrumentation, and operation of a laboratory-scale
two-stage metal hydride hydrogen compressor with exchangeable vessels. It is intended
to support reproducibility, model validation, and further analysis by the research
community.

The repository includes raw and processed measurement data, metadata describing the
experimental setup and sensors, and STEP files of the compressor components.


## Repository Structure
The repository is organized as follows:

- `Experiment Overview.xlsx`
  Overview of all datasets, organized by the 29 individual experiments.

- `Dataset/Experiment [1-29].xlsx`  
  Raw and processed datasets of each of the 29 experiments.

- `Stage-[I,II].STEP`  
  STEP files of the experimental compressor stages comprising the tank, heat exchanger, thermometer and connections.
  
- `LICENSE`  
  License file specifying reuse conditions for the dataset (CC BY 4.0).

- `README.txt`  
  This file.


## Experimental Context
The experiments were conducted on a two-stage metal hydride compressor operated under
controlled thermal and pressure boundary conditions. Different hydride material
combinations and operating parameters were investigated.

The dataset includes measurements such as temperatures, pressures, hydrogen mass
flows, and derived quantities used for mass and heat balance evaluations.

Details on the compressor design, instrumentation, and operating strategy are provided
in the associated journal publication.


## Data Usage and Reproducibility
The data are provided to enable:
- independent verification of the results reported in the paper,
- validation of numerical and analytical compressor models,
- comparative studies on metal hydride compressor performance and other analysis.

Users are responsible for interpreting the data correctly and for considering the
limitations discussed in the associated publication.
